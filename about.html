Our story

Made by hand,
meant to keep.

Oipuloh makes hand-crocheted flower bouquets as a long-lasting alternative to fresh flowers.

The brand

A quieter kind of flower.

Fresh flowers are beautiful, but they fade, drop petals, and need constant care. Oipuloh was built around a simple alternative: a bouquet that is finished by hand and ready to stay.

Each bouquet is crocheted from soft cotton yarn, assembled in full, and wrapped with paper and ribbon. No assembly is required when it arrives. There is no water to change and no petals to sweep up.

The collection includes seven floral styles, from bright daisies to layered peonies. Each one is designed to sit on a desk, shelf, nightstand, dining table, or entryway — and to look the same next week as it does today.

The process

From yarn to a finished bouquet.

  1. 01

    Stitch

    Blooms are crocheted from soft cotton yarn, petal by petal.

  2. 02

    Shape

    Each flower is shaped and assembled into a finished arrangement.

  3. 03

    Wrap

    The bouquet is wrapped in paper and finished with a ribbon.

  4. 04

    Gift

    A gift bag and greeting card are added, so it is ready to hand over.

What we care about

Three simple things.

Craft
Hand-crocheted blooms with a real textile texture — not printed or molded plastic.
Longevity
A bouquet that needs no water and keeps its shape, season after season.
Readiness
Gift bag and greeting card included, so the bouquet is complete when it arrives.
