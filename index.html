Everything you might want to know before choosing a bouquet.

Ask us something else →
Are the flowers real?

They are hand-crocheted from soft cotton yarn — not fresh, dried, or preserved flowers. The texture is textile, and the shape holds.

Do they need water or sunlight?

No. The bouquets need no watering, sunlight, pruning, or cleanup. Keep them wherever you would place a small vase.

What is included?

One finished bouquet, one gift bag, and one greeting card. The bouquet arrives assembled — it is not a DIY kit.

Will mine look exactly like the photo?

Because each bouquet is handmade, stitch detail, flower shape, and color may vary slightly from the photos. That variation is part of the handmade finish.

Where can I buy?
